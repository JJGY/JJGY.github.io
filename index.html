About Me

I am an Environmental Economist and Co-Founder of Ecosonus, working at the intersection of biodiversity, artificial intelligence, data science, and environmental decision-making.

I bring extensive international experience from the United Nations, where I managed multi-million-dollar environmental and development portfolios, led complex international programmes, mobilized resources, and worked with governments, international organizations, and multidisciplinary teams across countries.

My background combines Environmental Economics, Data Science, Ecology, and Food Technology & Engineering. Today, I apply this interdisciplinary expertise to develop scalable solutions for biodiversity, climate resilience, sustainable food systems, and responsible AI.

Ecosonus

Nature Tech for biodiversity monitoring and environmental intelligence

I co-founded Ecosonus, a nature-tech initiative developing AI-powered solutions for biodiversity monitoring and environmental decision-making.

Our work combines bioacoustics, remote sensing, geospatial modelling, artificial intelligence, and ecological data to generate actionable insights for conservation, ecosystem management, climate resilience, and nature-based tourism.

Selected Work

Selected projects at the intersection of biodiversity, climate, artificial intelligence, geospatial analysis, and environmental decision-making.

Strengthening Climate Resilience through Forest-Based Risk Reduction in Peru

This project links forest conservation with disaster risk reduction in Peru’s Yanachaga-Chemillén National Park and surrounding communities.

Using Google AlphaEarth and Sentinel satellite data, it identifies and monitors forest areas that help reduce landslide and flood risks and integrates ecosystem and risk information into decision-support tools.

Second Place, 2nd Copernicus Latin America Hackathon, European Union — 2025.

Multimodal AI for Acoustic Bird Species Identification

A multimodal AI application designed to support biodiversity conservation and nature-based tourism in remote, species-rich environments.

  • Bird identification using BirdNET-based models
  • Transfer learning for local and endemic species
  • Agentic LLM biodiversity assistant
  • Bioacoustic and ecological data integration
  • Remote sensing and geospatial visualization
Earlier Data Science Projects

Time Series Prediction of Outbreaks of Infectious Diseases

Predictive modelling of infectious disease outbreaks using SMA, ARMA, RNN, and LSTM models together with digital-stream data such as Google search trends.

Deep learning models outperformed traditional baselines in forecasting COVID-19 cases.

Impact of COVID-19 on Education

Analysis of the impact of COVID-19 on global primary education completion rates using Linear, Lasso, k-NN, and AdaBoost regression models.

UNESCO and World Bank data were used to estimate counterfactual 2020 outcomes without the pandemic.
